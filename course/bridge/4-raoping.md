# Bridge 4 · From Sixian to Raoping 饒平

Raoping is the scattered accent: pockets in 桃園, 新竹, 卓蘭 and 彰化, and in some places it is now spoken only at home
(MOE dictionary, article 107_002). The certification lists give one standard form. To a Sixian ear it is close to
Hailu with other tones: the same zh, ch, sh, rh, and the same vowels in most words.

**Measured.** 1,235 of the 1,314 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (94%); 79 are another word.

## 1. Tones

| Sixian | Raoping | How regular | Examples (Sixian → Raoping) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陰平 ˊ 24 | **ˇ 11** | 97% of 3,801 | 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˋ teˇ · 歡迎 fonˊ ngiangˇ → fanˇ ngiang · 坐 coˊ → coˇ |
| 陽平 ˇ 11 | **(no mark) 55** | 99% of 3,104 | 來 loiˇ → loi · 佢 giˇ → gi · 你 nˇ → ngi |
| 上聲 ˋ 31 | **ˋ 53** | 98% of 2,683 | 講 gongˋ → gongˋ · 這 iaˋ → liˋ · 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˋ teˇ |
| 去聲 55 | **ˋ 53** | 60% of 4,119 (also ˊ 24 36%) | 姓 xiang → siangˋ · 麼个 maˋ ge → maˋ gaiˋ · 愛 oi → oiˋ |
| 陰入 ˋ 2 | **ˋ 2 (short)** | 99% of 1,920 | 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˋ faˊ · 伯姆 bagˋ meˊ → bagˋ mi · 一 idˋ → rhidˋ |
| 陽入 5 | **(no mark) 5 (short)** | 98% of 1,173 | 食 siid → shed · 十 siib → shib · 粒 liab → liab |

**Which 去聲?** After b, d, g, z, j, it is ˋ 53 in 96% of 1,131 syllables (个 ge → gaiˋ · 放 biong → biongˋ · 做 zo → zoˋ). After other consonants both tones occur and the word has to be learned: after p, t, k, c, q or m, n, ng, l, ˊ 24 has 56% of 1,727 (就 qiu → ciuˊ · 二 ngi → ngiˊ · 大 tai → taiˊ).

Learn the row, not the mark: three of the marks are the Hailu ones with other values. The two short tones do not
change places as they do in Hailu: Sixian ˋ 2 stays low and unmarked 5 stays high. 上聲 and one half of 去聲 are both
ˋ 53.

## 2. Consonants

| Sixian | Raoping | Syllables | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| s | **sh** 49%, s 42%, **f** 8% | 1,703 | 食 siid → shed · 十 siib → shib · 做事 zo se → zoˋ sheˊ |
| (no initial) | **rh** 51%, (none) 39%, **v** 8% | 1,053 | 又 iu → rhiuˊ · 一 idˋ → rhidˋ · 有 iuˊ → rhiuˇ |
| x | **s** 100% | 519 | 姓 xiang → siangˋ · 四 xi → siˋ · 先生 xinˊ sangˊ → sinˇ senˇ |
| z | **zh** 51%, z 49% | 956 | 隻 zagˋ → zhagˋ · 轉去 zonˋ hi → zhanˋ kiuˋ · 豬肉 zuˊ ngiugˋ → zhuˇ ngiugˋ |
| c | **ch** 53%, c 46% | 888 | 火車 foˋ caˊ → foˋ chaˇ · 蟲仔 cungˇ eˋ → chung erˋ · 著 cog → chog |
| q | **c** 99% | 336 | 就 qiu → ciuˊ · 錢 qienˇ → cien · 七 qidˋ → cidˋ |
| j | **z** 99% | 300 | 阿姊 aˊ jiˋ → aˇ ze · 靚 jiangˊ → ziangˇ · 煎 jienˊ → zienˇ |
| h | h 87%, **k** 12% | 848 | 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˋ faˊ · 去 hi → kiuˋ · 口 heuˋ → kieuˋ |

- **z, c, s: Mandarin tells you.** Where Mandarin has zh, ch, sh or r, Sixian z, c, s is zh, ch, sh in 70% of 2,174 syllables (食 siid → shed · 十 siib → shib · 隻 zagˋ → zhagˋ). Where Mandarin has z, c, s, j, q or x, it stays z, c, s in 92% of 924.
- **j, q, x are written z, c, s** (99% of 1,155): 姓 xiang → siangˋ · 就 qiu → ciuˊ · 四 xi → siˋ. The sound before i is close; the spelling changes.
- **A Sixian syllable that starts with i** starts with **rh** in 84% of 637 syllables: 又 iu → rhiuˊ · 一 idˋ → rhidˋ · 有 iuˊ → rhiuˇ.
- **A Sixian syllable that starts with i** starts with **v** in 14% of 637 syllables: 花園 faˊ ienˇ → faˇ vien · 雨 iˋ → vuˋ · 圓 ienˇ → vien.
- **Sixian h or f where Mandarin has k or q** is **k** in 59% of 215 syllables: 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˋ faˊ · 去 hi → kiuˋ · 衫褲 samˊ fu → samˇ kuˋ.
- **s → f** in 142 syllables: 睡 soi → feˊ · 汽水 hi suiˋ → hiˋ fiˋ · 脣 sunˇ → fin.

Everything on the Hailu page applies. Raoping adds three things. k, as in Dapu: 去 hi → kiuˋ, 褲 fu → kuˋ,
客 hagˋ → kagˋ. v where Sixian starts with i: 雨 iˋ → vuˋ, 圓 ienˇ → vien (the examples all start with yu- in
Mandarin). And f in 水 fiˋ and 睡 feˊ.

Three rows carry most of the difference: ai → e (弟 taiˊ → teˇ, 矮 aiˋ → eˋ), on → an (碗 vonˋ → vanˋ) and
ui → i (水 suiˋ → fiˋ). 食 is shed, not shid: half of the Sixian iid syllables are ed in Raoping.

## 4. The suffix 仔

Of the 766 Sixian list words that end in 仔:

- **keeps 仔**: 602 (79%). 一息仔 idˋ xid eˋ → 一息仔 rhidˋ sid erˋ · 柑仔 gamˊ eˋ → 柑仔 gamˇ erˋ · 雞仔 gieˊ eˋ → 雞仔 gieˇ erˋ
- **drops it**: 134 (17%). 辣椒仔 lad zeuˊ eˋ → 辣椒 lad ziauˇ · 餅仔 biangˋ eˇ → 餅 biangˋ · 襪仔 madˋ eˇ → 襪 madˋ
- **another word**: 30 (4%). 手錶仔 suˋ beuˋ eˇ → 時錶 shi biauˋ · 筷仔 kuai eˋ → 箸 chuˊ · 吊菜仔 diau coi eˋ → 茄 kiau

Raoping keeps the suffix more often than Hailu does, and reads it erˋ.

## 6. The same eight sentences

From 客語百句 (客家委員會), where every sentence is recorded in six accents.

**1. Is anyone home?**

- Sixian: 有人在屋下無？ · iuˊ nginˇ toˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˇ?
- Raoping: 有人在屋下無？ · rhiiuˇ ngin doˇ vugˋ haˇ mo?

**2. Wash your hands before you eat.**

- Sixian: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。
- Raoping: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · shed ponˊ cien oiˋ senˇ seˋ shiuˋ。

**3. You're wearing so little: aren't you cold?**

- Sixian: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ seuˋ，voi honˇ moˇ?
- Raoping: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ shauˋ，voiˊ hon mo?

**4. This skirt is very pretty.**

- Sixian: 這條裙當靚。 · iaˋ tiauˇ kiunˇ dongˊ jiangˊ。
- Raoping: 這條裙當靚。 · liˋ tiau kiun dongˇ ziangˇ。

**5. Thank you!**

- Sixian: 恁仔細！ · anˋ ziiˋ se！
- Raoping: 承蒙你！ · siin mung ngi！

**6. The weather is good today.**

- Sixian: 今晡日天時異好。 · gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ tienˊ siiˇ i hoˋ。
- Raoping: 今晡日天時異好。 · gimˇ buˇ ngidˋ tienˇ shi iˋ hoˋ 。

**7. This road is shorter; that road is longer.**

- Sixian: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · iaˋ tiauˇ lu ka kiunˊ ， ge tiauˇ lu ka ienˋ。
- Raoping: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · liˋ tiau luˊ kaˋ kiunˇ ， gai tiau luˊ kaˋ vienˋ 。

**8. He is wearing new trousers to go to his grandmother's (his mother's mother).**

- Sixian: 佢著新褲去姐婆屋下。 · giˇ zogˋ xinˊ fu hi jiaˋ poˇ vugˋ kaˊ。
- Raoping: 佢著新褲去姐嬤屋下。 · gi zhogˋ sinˇ kuˋ kiuˋ ziaˋ ma vugˋ haˇ 。

Sentence 8: 去 is kiuˋ and 褲 is kuˋ, and the grandmother is 姐嬤. Sentence 7: 遠 is vienˋ. Sentence 5: 承蒙你, as in
Hailu.
