# Bridge 3 · From Sixian to Dapu 大埔

Dapu is spoken in 臺中 (東勢, 石岡, 新社, 和平) and in 卓蘭 in 苗栗 (MOE dictionary, article 107_002). Its consonants are
close to Hailu (zh, ch, sh, rh), its tones are its own, and it has no 仔: a word stands alone, or changes its tone.

**Measured.** 1,163 of the 1,313 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (89%); 150 are another word.

## 1. Tones

| Sixian | Dapu | How regular | Examples (Sixian → Dapu) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陰平 ˊ 24 | **+ 33** | 97% of 3,181 | 吾 ngaˊ → nga+ · 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˆ te+ · 歡迎 fonˊ ngiangˇ → fan+ ngiangˇ |
| 陽平 ˇ 11 | **ˇ 113** | 99% of 2,551 | 來 loiˇ → loiˇ · 佢 giˇ → giˇ · 你 nˇ → hnˇ |
| 上聲 ˋ 31 | **ˆ 31** | 98% of 2,260 | 講 gongˋ → gongˆ · 這 iaˋ → liaˆ · 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˆ te+ |
| 去聲 55 | **ˋ 53** | 96% of 3,510 | 係 he → heˋ · 姓 xiang → siangˋ · 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˆ faˋ |
| 陰入 ˋ 2 | **ˆ 21 (short)** | 98% of 1,563 | 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˆ faˋ · 伯姆 bagˋ meˊ → bagˆ me+ · 一 idˋ → rhidˆ |
| 陽入 5 | **ˋ 54 (short)** | 99% of 922 | 食 siid → shidˋ · 十 siib → shibˋ · 粒 liab → liabˋ |

This is the most regular table of the five pages: one Sixian tone, one Dapu tone, and 去聲 does not split. Two
shapes are new. + 33 is level and mid, where Sixian rises. ˇ 113 is low and rises a little at the end. The lists
write 去聲 with ˋ; the MOE dictionary leaves it unmarked.

## 2. Consonants

| Sixian | Dapu | Syllables | Examples |

| s | **sh** 59%, s 41% | 1,440 | 食 siid → shidˋ · 睡目 soi mugˋ → shoiˋ mugˆ · 十 siib → shibˋ |

| x | **s** 99% | 433 | 姓 xiang → siangˋ · 四 xi → siˋ · 先生 xinˊ sangˊ → sin+ sen+ |
| z | **zh** 51%, z 49% | 817 | 隻 zagˋ → zhagˆ · 轉去 zonˋ hi → zhonˆ ki+ · 豬肉 zuˊ ngiugˋ → zhu+ ngiugˆ |
| c | **ch** 53%, c 47% | 752 | 火車 foˋ caˊ → foˆ cha+ · 著 cog → chogˋ · 出 cudˋ → chudˆ |
| q | **c** 97% | 295 | 錢 qienˇ → cienˇ · 七 qidˋ → cidˆ · 青 qiangˊ → ciang+ |
| j | **z** 100% | 229 | 阿姊 aˊ jiˋ → a+ ziˆ · 靚 jiangˊ → ziang+ · 煎 jienˊ → zien+ |
| h | h 81%, **k** 19% | 711 | 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˆ faˋ · 去 hi → kiˋ · 汽水 hi suiˋ → kiˋ shuiˆ |

- **z, c, s: Mandarin tells you.** Where Mandarin has zh, ch, sh or r, Sixian z, c, s is zh, ch, sh in 77% of 1,880 syllables (食 siid → shidˋ · 睡目 soi mugˋ → shoiˋ mugˆ · 十 siib → shibˋ). Where Mandarin has z, c, s, j, q or x, it stays z, c, s in 93% of 768.
- **j, q, x are written z, c, s** (99% of 957): 姓 xiang → siangˋ · 四 xi → siˋ · 錢 qienˇ → cienˇ. The sound before i is close; the spelling changes.
- **A Sixian syllable that starts with i** starts with **rh** in 98% of 548 syllables: 又 iu → rhiuˋ · 一 idˋ → rhidˆ · 有 iuˊ → rhiu+.
- **Sixian h or f where Mandarin has k or q** is **k** in 91% of 171 syllables: 客話 hagˋ fa → kagˆ faˋ · 去 hi → kiˋ · 衫褲 samˊ fu → sam+ kuˋ.

zh, ch, sh and rh work as in Hailu. The new rule is k: 去 hi → kiˋ, 客 hagˋ → kagˆ, 褲 fu → kuˋ. 你 is hnˇ: a breath,
then n.

## 4. The suffix 仔

Of the 759 Sixian list words that end in 仔:

- **drops it**: 594 (78%). 柑仔 gamˊ eˋ → 柑 gam+ · 雞仔 gieˊ eˋ → 雞 gieˊ · 蟲仔 cungˇ eˋ → 蟲 chungˇ
- **another word**: 164 (22%). 一息仔 idˋ xid eˋ → 一屑屑 rhidˆ sudˋ sudˋ · 牙搓仔 ngaˇ co eˋ → 牙刷 ngaˇ sodˆ · 捽仔 cud eˋ → 捽布 cudˋ buˋ

Of the 594 words that drop 仔, 62 (10%) end in the rising tone ˊ 35, which no plain word has: 雞仔 gieˊ eˋ → 雞 gieˊ · 包仔 bauˊ eˋ → 包 bauˊ · 梯仔 toiˊ eˋ → 梯 toiˊ · 星仔 senˊ eˋ → 星 senˊ.

Dapu has no 仔. Most words only lose it. Some keep a trace: the last syllable takes the rising tone ˊ 35. When you
hear a rising tone in Dapu, think of a Sixian word in 仔.

## 6. The same eight sentences

From 客語百句 (客家委員會), where every sentence is recorded in six accents.

**1. Is anyone home?**

- Sixian: 有人在屋下無？ · iuˊ nginˇ toˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˇ?
- Dapu: 有人在屋家無？ · rhiu+ nginˇ to+ vugˆ ka+ moˇ?

**2. Wash your hands before you eat.**

- Sixian: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。
- Dapu: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · shidˋ ponˋ cienˇ oi+ sen+ seˆ shiuˆ。

**3. You're wearing so little: aren't you cold?**

- Sixian: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ seuˋ，voi honˇ moˇ?
- Dapu: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zhogˆ anˆ sheuˆ ，voi+ honˇ moˇ?

**4. This skirt is very pretty.**

- Sixian: 這條裙當靚。 · iaˋ tiauˇ kiunˇ dongˊ jiangˊ。
- Dapu: 這領裙當靚。 · liaˆ liang+ kiunˇ dong+ ziang+。

**5. Thank you!**

- Sixian: 恁仔細！ · anˋ ziiˋ se！
- Dapu: 勞瀝！ · loˇ ladˋ！

**6. The weather is good today.**

- Sixian: 今晡日天時異好。 · gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ tienˊ siiˇ i hoˋ。
- Dapu: 今晡日天時已好。 · gim+ bi+ ngidˆ tien+ shiˇ iˋ hoˆ 。

**7. This road is shorter; that road is longer.**

- Sixian: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · iaˋ tiauˇ lu ka kiunˊ ， ge tiauˇ lu ka ienˋ。
- Dapu: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · liaˆ tiauˇ luˋ ka+ kiun+ ， gaiˋ tiauˇ luˋ ka+ rhenˆ 。

**8. He is wearing new trousers to go to his grandmother's (his mother's mother).**

- Sixian: 佢著新褲去姐婆屋下。 · giˇ zogˋ xinˊ fu hi jiaˋ poˇ vugˋ kaˊ。
- Dapu: 佢著新褲去外阿婆屋家。 · giˇ zhogˆ sin+ kuˋ kiˋ nguaiˋ a+ poˇ vugˆ ka+。

Sentences 1 and 8: 屋家 ka+, not 屋下. Sentence 4: 領 is the classifier for the skirt, where Sixian has 條.
Sentence 8: 去 kiˋ and 褲 kuˋ, the k rule.
