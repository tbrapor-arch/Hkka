# Bridge · The other accents, starting from Sixian

Part 1 gave you Sixian with Hailu under every line. 進修 is Sixian only. Before it starts, these six pages show
how to get from the Sixian you know to the other accents of Taiwan: Hailu, South Sixian, Dapu, Raoping and Zhao'an.
The aim is to recognise them, not to speak them: to hear *shidˋ* or *shed* and know that it is 食 siid.

Every number, word and sentence on these pages is taken from the certification lists (115年度), the MOE dictionary
and 客語百句. No native speaker has checked the pages: the tables are counts, and the remarks under them are ours.

Where each accent is spoken (MOE dictionary, article 107_002):

- **Sixian** has the most speakers: 桃園, 新竹, 苗栗 in the north, 高雄 and 屏東 in the south, 花蓮 and 臺東 in the east.
- **Hailu** is second: 新竹, southern 桃園, northern 苗栗, 花蓮.
- **Dapu**: 臺中 (東勢, 石岡, 新社, 和平) and 卓蘭 in 苗栗.
- **Raoping**: in pockets in 桃園, 新竹, 卓蘭 and 彰化.
- **Zhao'an**: 雲林 (二崙, 崙背). It has the fewest speakers of the five.

## How far each accent is

- **Hailu 海陸**: 1,262 of the 1,314 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (96%); 52 are another word.
- **South Sixian 南四縣**: 13,222 of the 14,514 MOE dictionary entries with a southern reading are read exactly as in the north (91%).
- **Dapu 大埔**: 1,163 of the 1,313 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (89%); 150 are another word.
- **Raoping 饒平**: 1,235 of the 1,314 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (94%); 79 are another word.
- **Zhao'an 詔安**: 1,010 of the 1,313 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (77%); 303 are another word.

Read these figures as a ranking. For the four list accents the test is whether the word is written with the same
characters; a word with the same characters can still sound quite different. South Sixian is measured in another
way (same reading or not), because the certification lists have no southern column.

## The tones at a glance

| Sixian | Hailu | South Sixian | Dapu | Raoping | Zhao'an |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 陰平 ˊ 24 | ˋ 53 | ˊ 24 | + 33 | ˇ 11 | ˇ 11 |
| 陽平 ˇ 11 | (no mark) 55 | ˇ 11 | ˇ 113 | (no mark) 55 | ˋ 53 |
| 上聲 ˋ 31 | ˊ 24 | ˋ 31 | ˆ 31 | ˋ 53 | ˆ 31 |
| 去聲 55 | ˇ 11 | (no mark) 55 | ˋ 53 | ˋ 53 | ˆ 31 |
| 陰入 ˋ 2 | (no mark) 5 (short) | ˋ 2 (short) | ˆ 21 (short) | ˋ 2 (short) | ˊ 24 (short) |
| 陽入 5 | ˋ 2 (short) | (no mark) 5 (short) | ˋ 54 (short) | (no mark) 5 (short) | ˋ 43 (short) |

Read each row across: it is one tone category. A 陰平 word rises in Sixian (ˊ 24), falls in Hailu (ˋ 53), is level in
Dapu (+ 33) and low in Raoping and Zhao'an (ˇ 11). The marks are reused with other values from accent to accent, so
always read a mark with its accent. Dapu and Zhao'an write ˆ for their low falling tone; the lists type it ^.
Where a cell shows one tone, the page of that accent says how regular it is: 去聲 splits in two in Hailu, Raoping
and Zhao'an.

## One sentence, six accents

**1. Is anyone home?**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Sixian | 有人在屋下無？ | iuˊ nginˇ toˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˇ? |
| Hailu | 有人在屋下無？ | rhiuˋ ngin coiˋ vug haˋ mo? |
| South Sixian | 有人在屋下無？ | iuˊ nginˇ teˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˊ? |
| Dapu | 有人在屋家無？ | rhiu+ nginˇ to+ vugˆ ka+ moˇ? |
| Raoping | 有人在屋下無？ | rhiiuˇ ngin doˇ vugˋ haˇ mo? |
| Zhao'an | 有人在屋下無？ | rhiuˇ nginˋ da bbuˊ haˇ moˋ? |

**2. Wash your hands before you eat.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Sixian | 食飯前愛先洗手。 | siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。 |
| Hailu | 食飯前愛先洗手。 | shidˋ pon+ cien oiˇ senˋ seˊ shiuˊ。 |
| South Sixian | 食飯前愛先洗手。 | siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。 |
| Dapu | 食飯前愛先洗手。 | shidˋ ponˋ cienˇ oi+ sen+ seˆ shiuˆ。 |
| Raoping | 食飯前愛先洗手。 | shed ponˊ cien oiˋ senˇ seˋ shiuˋ。 |
| Zhao'an | 食飯前愛先洗手。 | shiedˋ pon cienˋ oiˆ sienˇ seˆ shiuˆ。 |
