# Bridge 2 · From Sixian to South Sixian 南四縣

South Sixian is the Sixian of the south: 高雄 and 屏東 (美濃 in the story, where 鍾伯 and his wife live). The MOE
dictionary gives it a column of its own. The certification lists do not, so the measure on this page is different:
how many dictionary entries have another reading in the south. Article 107_002 says only that north and south
differ in some sounds and some words. What you will notice first is a handful of very frequent words.

**Measured.** 13,222 of the 14,514 MOE dictionary entries with a southern reading are read exactly as in the north (91%).

## 1. Tones

| Sixian | South Sixian | How regular | Examples (Sixian → South Sixian) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陰平 ˊ 24 | **ˊ 24** | 99% of 6,276 | 吾 ngaˊ → ngaˊ · 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˋ taiˊ · 公 gungˊ → gungˊ |
| 陽平 ˇ 11 | **ˇ 11** | 100% of 5,360 | 你 nˇ → nˇ · 佢 giˇ → iˇ · 來 loiˇ → loiˇ |
| 上聲 ˋ 31 | **ˋ 31** | 99% of 4,347 | 講 gongˋ → gongˋ · 這 iaˋ → iaˋ · 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˋ taiˊ |
| 去聲 55 | **(no mark) 55** | 99% of 3,543 | 係 he → he · 姓 xiang → xiang · 會 voi → voi |
| 陰入 ˋ 2 | **ˋ 2 (short)** | 100% of 2,532 | 伯姆 bagˋ meˊ → bagˋ meˊ · 一 idˋ → idˋ · 粥 zugˋ → zugˋ |
| 陽入 5 | **(no mark) 5 (short)** | 99% of 837 | 食 siid → siid · 十 siib → siib · 粒 liab → liab |

The tones are the same six. The differences are single words. 無 at the end of a question is moˊ in the 客語百句
recordings (sentences 1 and 3 below), and a few words change tone (票 peuˊ → peu).

## 2. Consonants

No rule changes. Single words do: 你 is ngˇ in the recordings; 佢 is iˇ and 還 is vanˇ in the dictionary (the
recordings keep giˇ).

## 3. Vowels and endings

| Sixian | South Sixian | Syllables | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| ien | ien 54%, **ian** 46% | 1,006 | 間房 gienˊ fongˇ → gianˊ fongˇ · 花園 faˊ ienˇ → faˊ ianˇ · 前年 qienˇ ngienˇ → qienˇ ngianˇ |
| ied | **iad** 55%, ied 44% | 219 | 熱 ngied → ngiad · 歇睏 hiedˋ kun → hiadˋ kun · 結婚 giedˋ funˊ → giadˋ funˊ |
| ie | ie 48%, **iai** 48% | 93 | 街 gieˊ → giaiˊ · 解說 gieˋ sodˋ → giaiˋ sodˋ · 介 gie → giai |

One rule covers most of the differences: after i, the vowel e opens to a in about half of the words. ien → ian
(年 ngienˇ → ngianˇ, 園 ienˇ → ianˇ), ied → iad (熱 ngied → ngiad), ie → iai (街 gieˊ → giaiˊ).

## 4. The suffix 仔

仔 is eˋ, as in the north. In a few of the parallel sentences the south has no 仔 where the north has one
(揹書包仔 → 揹書包).

## 5. Words that change

| Sixian | South Sixian | |
|---|---|---|
| 當 dongˊ · 異 i | 蓋 goi | very (14 of the 101 sentences) |
| 較 ka | 過 go | more (before an adjective) (4 of the 101 sentences) |
| 恁仔細 anˋ ziiˋ se | 多謝 doˊ qia | thank you (1 of the 101 sentences) |
| 你 nˇ | 你 ngˇ | you (13 of the 101 sentences) |
| 在 toˊ | 在 teˊ | be at (6 of the 101 sentences · MOE) |
| 佢 giˇ | 佢 iˇ | he, she (MOE) |
| 還 hanˇ | 還 vanˇ | still (MOE) |
| 姐婆 jiaˋ poˇ | 外阿婆 ngoi aˊ poˇ | grandmother (mother's mother) (1 of the 101 sentences) |
| 餔娘 buˊ ngiongˇ | 姐仔 jiaˋ eˋ | wife (1 of the 101 sentences) |
| 這位 iaˋ vi | 這跡位 iaˋ jiagˋ vi | here (1 of the 101 sentences) |

蓋 is the word to remember: it stands before an adjective where the north has 當 or 異. 過 before an adjective is
"more", where the north has 較. The last column says where the southern form is found: in how many of the 101
southern sentences of 客語百句 that are intact in the data, or in the MOE dictionary.

## 6. The same eight sentences

From 客語百句 (客家委員會), where every sentence is recorded in six accents.

**1. Is anyone home?**

- Sixian: 有人在屋下無？ · iuˊ nginˇ toˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˇ?
- South Sixian: 有人在屋下無？ · iuˊ nginˇ teˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˊ?

**2. Wash your hands before you eat.**

- Sixian: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。
- South Sixian: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。

**3. You're wearing so little: aren't you cold?**

- Sixian: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ seuˋ，voi honˇ moˇ?
- South Sixian: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ seuˋ，voi honˇ moˊ?

**4. This skirt is very pretty.**

- Sixian: 這條裙當靚。 · iaˋ tiauˇ kiunˇ dongˊ jiangˊ。
- South Sixian: 這領裙蓋鬧。 · iaˋ liangˊ kiunˇ goi nau。

**5. Thank you!**

- Sixian: 恁仔細！ · anˋ ziiˋ se！
- South Sixian: 多謝！ · doˊ qia ！

**6. The weather is good today.**

- Sixian: 今晡日天時異好。 · gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ tienˊ siiˇ i hoˋ。
- South Sixian: 今晡日天時蓋好。 · gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ tienˊ siiˇ goi hoˋ 。

**7. This road is shorter; that road is longer.**

- Sixian: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · iaˋ tiauˇ lu ka kiunˊ ， ge tiauˇ lu ka ienˋ。
- South Sixian: 這條路過近，該條路過遠。 · iaˋ tiauˇ lu go kiunˊ ， ge tiauˇ lu go ianˋ 。

**8. He is wearing new trousers to go to his grandmother's (his mother's mother).**

- Sixian: 佢著新褲去姐婆屋下。 · giˇ zogˋ xinˊ fu hi jiaˋ poˇ vugˋ kaˊ。
- South Sixian: 佢著新褲去外阿婆屋下。 · giˇ zogˋ xinˊ fu hi ngoi aˊ poˇ vugˋ kaˊ 。

Sentence 4 holds the whole page in five syllables: 這領裙蓋鬧. 領 for 條, 蓋 for 當, 鬧 for 靚. Sentence 2 is the same
as in the north. Sentences 5 to 8 show the rest: 多謝, 蓋好, 過近 and 過遠, 外阿婆.
