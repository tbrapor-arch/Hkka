# Bridge 5 · From Sixian to Zhao'an 詔安

Zhao'an is spoken in 雲林 (二崙, 崙背), with smaller groups elsewhere, and has the fewest speakers of the five list
accents (MOE dictionary, article 107_002). It is the far end of the bridge: about one Part 1 word in four is another
word, and many short syllables have lost their ending. Its speakers live among Southern Min speakers, and many of
its everyday words are the Southern Min ones (general knowledge, not measured here). If you did Tsheh-tû, you
already know them.

**Measured.** 1,010 of the 1,313 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (77%); 303 are another word.

## 1. Tones

| Sixian | Zhao'an | How regular | Examples (Sixian → Zhao'an) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陰平 ˊ 24 | **ˇ 11** | 91% of 2,224 (also ˆ 31 6%) | 歡迎 fonˊ ngiangˇ → fanˇ ngiangˋ · 坐 coˊ → cooˇ · 阿公 aˊ gungˊ → aˇ gong |
| 陽平 ˇ 11 | **ˋ 53** | 94% of 1,831 | 來 loiˇ → loiˋ · 佢 giˇ → guiˋ · 你 nˇ → henˋ |
| 上聲 ˋ 31 | **ˆ 31** | 92% of 1,517 | 講 gongˋ → gongˆ · 兩 liongˋ → liongˆ · 碗 vonˋ → bbanˆ |
| 去聲 55 | **ˆ 31** | 52% of 2,507 (also (no mark) 55 42%) | 姓 xiang → siangˆ · 麼个 maˋ ge → bbooˊ gaiˆ · 愛 oi → oiˆ |
| 陰入 ˋ 2 | **ˊ 24 (short)** | 59% of 1,103 (also ˊ 24 33%) | 一 idˋ → rhidˊ · 八 badˋ → beedˊ · 七 qidˋ → cidˊ |
| 陽入 5 | **ˋ 43 (short)** | 60% of 700 (also (no mark) 55 30%; ˊ 24 (short) 6%) | 食 siid → shiedˋ · 十 siib → shibˋ · 粒 liab → liabˋ |

**Which 去聲?** After b, d, g, z, j, it is ˆ 31 in 89% of 667 syllables (做 zo → zooˆ · 放 biong → biongˆ · 到 do → dooˆ). After other consonants both tones occur and the word has to be learned: after p, t, k, c, q or m, n, ng, l, (no mark) 55 has 64% of 1,069 (就 qiu → ciu · 二 ngi → ngi · 大 tai → tai).

陰平 is low (ˇ 11) and 陽平 falls (ˋ 53): the opposite of Sixian. 上聲 keeps its shape (31) and is written ˆ. The short
tones are the surprise: about a third of the Sixian short syllables have no stop in Zhao'an. They keep the tone and
lose the -g: 百 bagˋ → baˊ, 白 pag → pa, 落 log → loo.

## 2. Consonants

| Sixian | Zhao'an | Syllables | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| s | **sh** 50%, s 40%, **f** 9% | 1,040 | 食 siid → shiedˋ · 十 siib → shibˋ · 做事 zo se → zooˆ she |
| (no initial) | **rh** 53%, (none) 33%, **bb** 11% | 573 | 一 idˋ → rhidˊ · 有 iuˊ → rhiuˇ · 朋友 penˇ iuˊ → penˋ rhiuˆ |
| z | **zh** 52%, z 46% | 580 | 隻 zagˋ → zhaˊ · 轉去 zonˋ hi → zheenˆ kuiˆ · 豬肉 zuˊ ngiugˋ → zhiˇ ngiuˊ |
| c | **ch** 52%, c 42% | 540 | 火車 foˋ caˊ → fooˆ chaˇ · 著 cog → choo · 出 cudˋ → chidˊ |
| x | **s** 97% | 309 | 姓 xiang → siangˆ · 四 xi → siˆ · 先生 xinˊ sangˊ → sienˇ senˇ |
| v | **bb** 89% | 229 | 會 voi → bbue · 碗 vonˋ → bbanˆ · 屋下 vugˋ kaˊ → bbuˊ haˇ |
| q | **c** 97% | 221 | 就 qiu → ciu · 錢 qienˇ → cienˋ · 七 qidˋ → cidˊ |
| j | **z** 96% | 171 | 阿姊 aˊ jiˋ → aˇ ziˆ · 靚 jiangˊ → ziangˇ · 煎 jienˊ → zienˇ |
| h | h 76%, **k** 21% | 495 | 去 hi → kuiˆ · 汽水 hi suiˋ → kiˆ fiˆ · 口 heuˋ → keuˆ |
| ng | ng 89%, **n** 10% | 519 | 前年 qienˇ ngienˇ → cienˋ neenˋ · 屙尿 oˊ ngiau → ooˇ neeu · 唸 ngiam → neem |

- **z, c, s: Mandarin tells you.** Where Mandarin has zh, ch, sh or r, Sixian z, c, s is zh, ch, sh in 72% of 1,374 syllables (食 siid → shiedˋ · 十 siib → shibˋ · 隻 zagˋ → zhaˊ). Where Mandarin has z, c, s, j, q or x, it stays z, c, s in 92% of 581.
- **j, q, x are written z, c, s** (99% of 701): 姓 xiang → siangˆ · 就 qiu → ciu · 四 xi → siˆ. The sound before i is close; the spelling changes.
- **A Sixian syllable that starts with i** starts with **rh** in 78% of 388 syllables: 一 idˋ → rhidˊ · 有 iuˊ → rhiuˇ · 朋友 penˇ iuˊ → penˋ rhiuˆ.
- **A Sixian syllable that starts with i** starts with **bb** in 16% of 388 syllables: 花園 faˊ ienˇ → faˇ bbienˋ · 雨 iˋ → bbuˆ · 圓 ienˇ → bbienˋ.
- **Sixian h or f where Mandarin has k or q** is **k** in 98% of 123 syllables: 去 hi → kuiˆ · 衫褲 samˊ fu → samˇ kuˆ · 汽水 hi suiˋ → kiˆ fiˆ.
- **v → bb** in 89% of 229 syllables: 會 voi → bbue · 碗 vonˋ → bbanˆ · 屋下 vugˋ kaˊ → bbuˊ haˇ.
- **ng → n** in 10% of 519 syllables: 前年 qienˇ ngienˇ → cienˋ neenˋ · 屙尿 oˊ ngiau → ooˇ neeu · 唸 ngiam → neem.
- **s → f** in 95 syllables: 睡 soi → fe · 汽水 hi suiˋ → kiˆ fiˆ · 脣 sunˇ → finˋ.

zh, ch, sh and rh work as in Hailu, k as in Dapu and Raoping (去 kuiˆ, 褲 kuˆ), f as in Raoping (水 fiˆ, 睡 fe).
The new sound is bb, a voiced b. It stands where Sixian has v (會 voi → bbue, 碗 vonˋ → bbanˆ) and where Raoping has
v (雨 iˋ → bbuˆ, 圓 ienˇ → bbienˋ). 你 is henˋ and 佢 is guiˋ.

oo is the open o, and most Sixian o are oo in Zhao'an. The short syllables in -g lose it: og → oo, ag → a, ug → u.
ien is een in a third of the syllables (天 tienˊ → teenˇ), and ii is i or u (事情 sii qinˇ → su cinˋ).

## 4. The suffix 仔

Of the 717 Sixian list words that end in 仔:

- **drops it**: 334 (47%). 雞仔 gieˊ eˋ → 雞 geˇ · 蟲仔 cungˇ eˋ → 蟲 chungˋ · 餅仔 biangˋ eˇ → 餅 biangˆ
- **another word**: 303 (42%). 辣椒仔 lad zeuˊ eˋ → 薟椒 hiamˇ ziooˇ · 牙搓仔 ngaˇ co eˋ → 牙筅 ngaˋ cingˆ · 捽仔 cud eˋ → 樹奶捽 shi neenˇ cudˋ
- **has 子**: 58 (8%). 柑仔 gamˊ eˋ → 柑子 gamˇ zuˆ · 包仔 bauˊ eˋ → 包子 bauˇ zuˆ · 細妹仔 se moi eˋ → 妹子 moiˆ zuˆ
- **keeps 仔**: 22 (3%). 一息仔 idˋ xid eˋ → 一兜仔 rhidˊ deuˇ aˋ · 一滴仔 idˋ did eˋ → 一兜仔 rhidˊ deuˇ aˋ · 側側仔 zedˋ zedˋ eˇ → 側側仔 zedˊ zedˊ aˋ

Zhao'an has almost no 仔. Half of the words lose it, and some take 子 zuˆ in its place (柑子, 包子, 妹子). Where the
list keeps 仔, it is read aˋ.

## 6. The same eight sentences

From 客語百句 (客家委員會), where every sentence is recorded in six accents.

**1. Is anyone home?**

- Sixian: 有人在屋下無？ · iuˊ nginˇ toˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˇ?
- Zhao'an: 有人在屋下無？ · rhiuˇ nginˋ da bbuˊ haˇ moˋ?

**2. Wash your hands before you eat.**

- Sixian: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。
- Zhao'an: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · shiedˋ pon cienˋ oiˆ sienˇ seˆ shiuˆ。

**3. You're wearing so little: aren't you cold?**

- Sixian: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ seuˋ，voi honˇ moˇ?
- Zhao'an: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zhooˊ ngidˆ shioˆ ，bbue honˋ moˋ?

**4. This skirt is very pretty.**

- Sixian: 這條裙當靚。 · iaˋ tiauˇ kiunˇ dongˊ jiangˊ。
- Zhao'an: 這領裙真靚。 · liˆ liangˇ kunˋ zhinˇ ziangˇ。

**5. Thank you!**

- Sixian: 恁仔細！ · anˋ ziiˋ se！
- Zhao'an: 勞力！ · loˋ ladˋ！

**6. The weather is good today.**

- Sixian: 今晡日天時異好。 · gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ tienˊ siiˇ i hoˋ。
- Zhao'an: 今日天氣真好。 · ginˇ ngidˊ teenˇ kiˆ zhinˇ hooˆ。

**7. This road is shorter; that road is longer.**

- Sixian: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · iaˋ tiauˇ lu ka kiunˊ ， ge tiauˇ lu ka ienˋ。
- Zhao'an: 這條路較近，遐條路較遠。 · liˆ teeuˋ lu kaˆ kunˇ ， gaˊ teeuˋ lu kaˆ bbienˆ。

**8. He is wearing new trousers to go to his grandmother's (his mother's mother).**

- Sixian: 佢著新褲去姐婆屋下。 · giˇ zogˋ xinˊ fu hi jiaˋ poˇ vugˋ kaˊ。
- Zhao'an: 佢著新褲去外媽屋下。 · guiˋ zhooˊ sinˇ kuˆ kuiˆ ngnai maˆ bbuˊ haˇ。

Sentence 1: 屋 is bbuˊ, with no -g. Sentence 3: 著 is zhooˊ, the same loss. Sentence 6: 今日 and 天氣, and 真 for
"very". Sentence 7: 遐 gaˊ for "that". Sentence 5: 勞力.
