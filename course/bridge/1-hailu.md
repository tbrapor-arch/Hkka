# Bridge 1 · From Sixian to Hailu 海陸

You have read Hailu under every Sixian line for 100 lessons, and lesson 0 gave you the tone code. This page checks
that code against the whole certification list and adds the rules for consonants and vowels. Hailu is spoken in
新竹, southern 桃園, northern 苗栗 and 花蓮, and has the second largest number of speakers (MOE dictionary, article
107_002). In the story, 阿舅 in 竹東 speaks it.

**Measured.** 1,262 of the 1,314 Part 1 words in the lists are written with the same characters (96%); 52 are another word.

## 1. Tones

| Sixian | Hailu | How regular | Examples (Sixian → Hailu) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陰平 ˊ 24 | **ˋ 53** | 96% of 3,648 | 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˊ taiˋ · 歡迎 fonˊ ngiangˇ → fonˋ ngiang · 坐 coˊ → coˋ |
| 陽平 ˇ 11 | **(no mark) 55** | 99% of 2,997 | 來 loiˇ → loi · 佢 giˇ → gi · 你 nˇ → ngi |
| 上聲 ˋ 31 | **ˊ 24** | 97% of 2,595 | 講 gongˋ → gongˊ · 這 iaˋ → liaˊ · 老弟 loˋ taiˊ → loˊ taiˋ |
| 去聲 55 | **ˇ 11** | 62% of 4,031 (also + 33 35%) | 係 he → heˇ · 姓 xiang → siangˇ · 麼个 maˋ ge → maˊ gaiˇ |
| 陰入 ˋ 2 | **(no mark) 5 (short)** | 98% of 1,847 | 客話 hagˋ fa → hag voiˋ · 伯姆 bagˋ meˊ → bag meˊ · 一 idˋ → rhid |
| 陽入 5 | **ˋ 2 (short)** | 93% of 1,089 (also (no mark) 5 (short) 7%) | 食 siid → shidˋ · 十 siib → shibˋ · 辣 lad → ladˋ |

**Which 去聲?** After b, d, g, z, j, it is ˇ 11 in 97% of 1,095 syllables (个 ge → gaiˇ · 放 biong → biongˇ · 做 zo → zoˇ). After other consonants both tones occur and the word has to be learned: after p, t, k, c, q or m, n, ng, l, + 33 has 55% of 1,688 (就 qiu → ciu+ · 二 ngi → ngi+ · 大 tai → tai+).

Five of the six Sixian tones give one Hailu tone almost every time. The code is a swap: rising and falling change
places (ˊ 24 → ˋ 53, ˋ 31 → ˊ 24), low becomes high (ˇ 11 → 55), and the two short tones change places. Only 去聲
needs the word.

## 2. Consonants

| Sixian | Hailu | Syllables | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| s | **sh** 58%, s 42% | 1,647 | 食 siid → shidˋ · 睡目 soi mugˋ → shoi+ mug · 十 siib → shibˋ |
| (no initial) | **rh** 59%, (none) 40% | 1,012 | 又 iu → rhiu+ · 一 idˋ → rhid · 有 iuˊ → rhiuˋ |
| x | **s** 100% | 497 | 姓 xiang → siangˇ · 四 xi → siˇ · 先生 xinˊ sangˊ → sinˋ sangˋ |
| z | **zh** 53%, z 47% | 929 | 隻 zagˋ → zhag · 轉去 zonˋ hi → zhonˊ hiˇ · 豬肉 zuˊ ngiugˋ → zhuˋ ngiug |
| c | **ch** 53%, c 46% | 864 | 火車 foˋ caˊ → foˊ chaˋ · 蟲仔 cungˇ eˋ → chung er · 著 cog → chogˋ |
| q | **c** 98% | 332 | 就 qiu → ciu+ · 錢 qienˇ → cien · 七 qidˋ → cid |
| j | **z** 100% | 275 | 阿姊 aˊ jiˋ → a+ ze · 靚 jiangˊ → ziangˋ · 煎 jienˊ → zienˋ |

- **z, c, s: Mandarin tells you.** Where Mandarin has zh, ch, sh or r, Sixian z, c, s is zh, ch, sh in 77% of 2,130 syllables (食 siid → shidˋ · 睡目 soi mugˋ → shoi+ mug · 十 siib → shibˋ). Where Mandarin has z, c, s, j, q or x, it stays z, c, s in 93% of 885.
- **j, q, x are written z, c, s** (99% of 1,104): 姓 xiang → siangˇ · 就 qiu → ciu+ · 四 xi → siˇ. The sound before i is close; the spelling changes.
- **A Sixian syllable that starts with i** starts with **rh** in 98% of 611 syllables: 又 iu → rhiu+ · 一 idˋ → rhid · 有 iuˊ → rhiuˋ.

Hailu has zh, ch, sh and rh; Sixian has none of them. For z, c, s, your Mandarin does most of the work. rh is the
buzzed y of lesson 0: where Sixian starts a syllable with i, Hailu has rh, and the i is often gone (樣 iong → rhong+).

Most of these rows follow from the consonants. After zh, ch, sh the vowel ii is i (食 siid → shidˋ, 時 siiˇ → shi),
and after rh the i is dropped (iong → ong, ia → a, ien → an). Three rows are worth learning as they are:
u → iu (手 suˋ → shiuˊ), eu → iau or au (笑 seu → siauˇ, 少 seuˋ → shauˊ) and e → ai (个 ge → gaiˇ).

## 4. The suffix 仔

Of the 765 Sixian list words that end in 仔:

- **keeps 仔**: 446 (58%). 一息仔 idˋ xid eˋ → 一息仔 rhid sid er+ · 柑仔 gamˊ eˋ → 柑仔 gamˋ er · 雞仔 gieˊ eˋ → 雞仔 gaiˋ er
- **drops it**: 264 (35%). 餅仔 biangˋ eˇ → 餅 biangˊ · 襪仔 madˋ eˇ → 襪 mad · 牛仔 ngiuˇ eˋ → 牛 ngiu
- **another word**: 53 (7%). 手錶仔 suˋ beuˋ eˇ → 時錶 shi biauˊ · 筷仔 kuai eˋ → 箸 chu+ · 中中仔 zungˊ zungˊ eˋ → 中般 zhungˋ banˋ

Hailu 仔 is er (55). It does not change with the sound before it, as Sixian eˋ does. About one Sixian word in three
has no suffix in Hailu: 桌仔 → 桌, 狗仔 → 狗.

## 6. The same eight sentences

From 客語百句 (客家委員會), where every sentence is recorded in six accents.

**1. Is anyone home?**

- Sixian: 有人在屋下無？ · iuˊ nginˇ toˊ vugˋ kaˊ moˇ?
- Hailu: 有人在屋下無？ · rhiuˋ ngin coiˋ vug haˋ mo?

**2. Wash your hands before you eat.**

- Sixian: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · siid fan qienˇ oi xienˊ seˋ suˋ。
- Hailu: 食飯前愛先洗手。 · shidˋ pon+ cien oiˇ senˋ seˊ shiuˊ。

**3. You're wearing so little: aren't you cold?**

- Sixian: 著恁少，會寒無？ · zogˋ anˋ seuˋ，voi honˇ moˇ?
- Hailu: 著恁少，會冷無？ · zhog anˊ shauˊ，voi+ langˋ mo?

**4. This skirt is very pretty.**

- Sixian: 這條裙當靚。 · iaˋ tiauˇ kiunˇ dongˊ jiangˊ。
- Hailu: 這條裙當靚。 · liaˊ tiau kiun dongˋ ziangˋ。

**5. Thank you!**

- Sixian: 恁仔細！ · anˋ ziiˋ se！
- Hailu: 承蒙你！ · shin mung ngi！

**6. The weather is good today.**

- Sixian: 今晡日天時異好。 · gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ tienˊ siiˇ i hoˋ。
- Hailu: 今晡日天時已好。 · gimˋ buˋ ngid tienˋ shi iˋ hoˊ 。

**7. This road is shorter; that road is longer.**

- Sixian: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · iaˋ tiauˇ lu ka kiunˊ ， ge tiauˇ lu ka ienˋ。
- Hailu: 這條路較近，該條路較遠。 · liaˊ tiau lu+ haˇ kiunˋ ， gai tiau lu+ haˇ rhianˊ 。

**8. He is wearing new trousers to go to his grandmother's (his mother's mother).**

- Sixian: 佢著新褲去姐婆屋下。 · giˇ zogˋ xinˊ fu hi jiaˋ poˇ vugˋ kaˊ。
- Hailu: 佢著新褲去姐婆屋下。 · gi zhog sinˋ fuˇ hiˇ ziaˊ po vug haˋ 。

Sentences 1, 2, 4, 7 and 8 keep every word: only the sounds move. In sentence 3, 寒 is 冷. In sentence 5,
恁仔細 is 承蒙你. In sentence 7, 較 is read haˇ.
