# Batch 7 · Lessons 43–49 · January: a wedding, the fields, a quarrel

## The week
The first weeks of January 2027. Weddings crowd into the weeks before the Lunar New Year, and the neighbours of lesson
32 (阿財伯 and 財伯姆) send a red invitation: their grandson is marrying. Sam goes to his first 食酒 (the wedding
banquet that Grandma and Grandpa went to in lesson 27). Then a quiet week: Grandpa shows Sam and A-min the rice
fields and the vegetable plot; A-min orders New Year clothes online, with Sam's card; they quarrel, and make up.

Calendar: 43 Monday (the invitation, at breakfast); 44 Saturday (the banquet, evening; A-in is home); 45 Sunday
morning (the fields); 46 the following Tuesday evening (A-min after school); 47 Friday (the parcel and the bill
arrive); 48 Saturday; 49 review, Sunday. A-in works in Taipei on weekdays, A-min is at school on weekdays.
