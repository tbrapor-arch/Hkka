# Batch 6 · Lessons 36–42 · The end of the year

## The week
Lucie has gone home; the house is quiet and Sam, without an interpreter's job, is a learner again. The last ten days
of December, with the cold wave lingering. Grandma teaches Sam to make 擂茶 for visitors; Grandpa, at last, sings the
山歌 he was too shy to sing in lesson 20; Sam takes the family out to a restaurant to thank them for Lucie's week;
then he catches Lucie's cold and has to see a doctor; once better, he helps Grandpa prune the tea bushes; A-in comes
home upset after Grandma has arranged for her to "meet someone". The review is the last evening of the year.

| # | Scene | What the learner can say afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 36 | Making 擂茶 with Grandma | "have a go" ((試)V看啊), "quickly / a bit" (VV啊) |
| 37 | Grandpa sings 山歌 | everyday vs learned readings (生, 行, 白), songs |
| 38 | The restaurant | "one more" (食加一碗, …添), "too little" (著少一領衫), ordering |
| 39 | The doctor | 毋過 / 但係 "but", 恁 "so", 有 + adj vs 會 + adj |
| 40 | Pruning the tea bushes | 摎 + person "for someone", 跈 "follow", 共下做 "together" |
| 41 | A-in is angry | 歡喜, 譴, 煩惱, 驚; 嗄 "unexpectedly" |
| 42 | Review: the last evening of the year | nothing new |

## Threads
- Grandpa's 山歌 (lesson 20) pays off in lesson 37; Grandma reveals that this is how they met.
- Grandma's marriage campaign for A-in (lessons 16, 23, 31) comes to a head in lesson 41; the A-in and Sam rumour
  (lesson 32) is not resolved, only teased.
- Sam's cold: Lucie left it behind (lesson 34).
- Grandpa's jacket went to France with Lucie (lesson 34), so Grandpa is cold in the tea field.

## Voices
- Sam: back to making mistakes, now ambitious ones (he tries the learned reading where the everyday one is needed).
- A-in: for the first time openly cross with her grandmother; still polite in form.

21 new words per lesson (±3), at most 8 Hakka-only or false-friend words. Every new word should appear in the
dialogue where possible. Follow `checklist.md`.
