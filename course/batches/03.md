# Batch 3 · Lessons 15–21 · The first cold week; A-in comes home

## The week
The first cold front of the winter reaches Miaoli and Sam has packed for summer. The week builds towards Saturday:
A-in is coming back for the weekend (the first 轉來 in the course, after lesson 6's 轉去), Grandma cooks 客家小炒
for her, and at dinner Grandpa, for once, talks: about the old days. The review is Sunday morning, A-in and Sam
walking in the tea field, and she notices how much he understands.

## Threads carried on
- Grandpa's terseness (4, 9, 14) pays off in 20: when he talks at length, it is an event; the family goes quiet.
- 轉去 (6) → 轉來 (18). The family's "two homes".
- Mr Liu, so far offstage, gets a lesson (16): the tea business that made Grandpa's porch tea.
- Sam's false-friend accidents continue, one per lesson at most.

## Load
19 new words per lesson (±3), at most 6 Hakka-only or false-friend words. Batch 2 ran a little under target (190 for
198 planned); lessons 15–20 may take 20.
