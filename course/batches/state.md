## Log (from P36 on: date and scene, what changed, threads opened or closed)

  clothes. Sam says he must pass the university's exam before the 中級 test. They walk to town to see the lanterns.
- P37, Mon 5 – Sun 11 Mar, Sam's diary. New character: 上屋叔婆, an old woman who lives alone up his lane, on her

  into groups of four; his group will study together once a week.
- P38, Fri 16 Mar, 伯公生. Grandpa and Sam watch a comic piece at the shrine (roles: 阿旺, 阿旺姆, 大嫂, 阿秀; 阿香 is
  named). Not part of the family story.
- P39, Sat 24 Mar, A-in's radio slot. 阿舅's factory: started work on 年初六, three batches a day, an accountant, two
  shop assistants, an assistant. He wants 阿傑 as manager. Open day for children next month.
- P40, Sat 31 Mar, 黃醫生 (82) speaks at the old people's club in 公館. Grandpa was there (P42).
- P41, Sat 7 Apr. Grandpa, Sam and A-min at the old coal mine where 阿太 worked, then a waterfall (30 m). Rain on the
  way back, a rainbow in the east.
- P42 (review), Sun 15 Apr, lunch at the Liu house with 阿舅. 阿傑 is manager. Grandpa still smokes.
- P43, Sun 22 Apr, at the Liu house. Sam asks Grandpa and Grandma about beliefs and feast days for a class paper.
  Grandma asked the temple about Sam's exam: no yes from the blocks. 阿太 once survived a mine accident (invented).
  Sam's family is Catholic. 阿舅's open day is Sat 28 Apr.
- P44, Sat 28 Apr, A-in's radio slot: she interviews A-min about his school's contest week (plays Mon, choirs Wed,
  table tennis; a painter's talk on Fri). His class came second in the choir contest (thread of P30 closed); he got
  a fountain pen. The open day at 阿舅's factory is that afternoon.
- P45, Sat 28 Apr afternoon, the open day at 阿舅's factory in 竹東 (thread of P39 closed). Thirty-odd children;
  阿傑 guides, the master cooks noodles, nobody pays. A-min asks to work there in the summer: 阿舅 says yes, come
  and look around first.
- P46, Sun 6 May, Sam's letter to 鍾伯 and 鍾伯姆 (answers P32). His exam is "next week" (about 14–18 May). A power

- P47, Sat 19 May evening, Sam's room. His exam ended Fri 18 May (pass mark 70, result not known). 上屋叔婆 comes
