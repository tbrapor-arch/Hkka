# Batch 1 · Lessons 1–7 · Sam's first week in Miaoli

A batch is one review cycle (six lessons and the review). This brief is written before the lessons and updated after
the review pass; read it with `syllabus.yaml` before writing the next batch.

## The week
Sam comes down from Taipei on a Friday evening. A-in meets him at 苗栗 station with her brother A-min; the house, the
grandparents, the first breakfast, Grandpa's morning tea, the market with Mrs Liu. On Sunday evening A-in goes back to
Taipei for work, and Sam is left with a family that speaks Hakka. That departure (lesson 6) is the hinge of the batch:
from now on he has to cope. The review (lesson 7) is his first conversation alone with Grandma.

| # | Scene | What the learner can say afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 苗栗 station, Friday evening: A-in, A-min | thank someone, introduce people, say a surname, "a little" |
| 2 | The Liu house: Grandpa, Grandma, Mr and Mrs Liu | name the family, "this / that", "my / your / his" |
| 3 | Saturday breakfast, Grandma's 食飽吂？ | the greeting and its answers, "want to", "not yet" |
| 4 | Grandpa's tea on the porch | "is there…?", "he's (doing)…", yes/no questions with 無 |
| 5 | The market with Mrs Liu | numbers, prices, the three everyday classifiers |
| 6 | Sunday: A-in's train back | days and hours, coming and going, "what for?" |
| 7 | Review: Monday, Sam and Grandma alone | nothing new |

## Voices
- **Sam**: polite, game, over-prepared. Has learned 恁仔細 on the train and uses it too much. Mandarin habits leak.
- **A-in** (25): warm, quick, his friend from Taipei; the only one who would switch to Mandarin, and she won't.
- **A-min** (14): blunt, curious, laughs at Sam's tones, secretly proud to be teacher.
- **Grandma** (阿婆): Hakka only, feeds people, asks the same questions every day.
- **Grandpa** (阿公): few words, tea, the porch.
- **Mrs Liu** (阿姆): practical, runs the kitchen and the market list. **Mr Liu** (阿爸): mostly at the tea business.

## Running threads (to pick up later)
- Sam's 恁仔細 for everything (1 →).
- Grandma's daily 食飽吂？ (3 →), whatever the hour.
- A-min correcting Sam's tones (1 →).
- False friends staged as small misunderstandings rather than only notes: 愛 (3), 該 (2), 當 (8), 走 (13).

## Constraints
- 16 new words per lesson (±3), at most 6 Hakka-only or false-friend words (`pacing.md`).
- Every word taught before use (lint); names (苗栗, 阿英, 阿明, Sam) exempt.
- Hailu written alongside with Hailu words: 承蒙 for 恁仔細, 𠊎 + kin for 吾 + kin, 个 gaiˇ, 這 liaˊ.
