# Batch 8 · Lessons 50–56 · The Lunar New Year

## The week
Early February 2027: New Year's Eve is Friday 5 February, 初一 Saturday 6. The syllabus put Sam's month in 竹東 here;
the year's biggest festival comes first, and it leads there. Grandma and Mrs Liu make the New Year 粄; the family sits
down to 圍爐 on New Year's Eve; 初一 brings visitors; on 初二 Mrs Liu goes back to her family (轉妹家) in 竹東, who
speak Hailu, and her brother offers Sam a month's work there; a walk down old 竹東 street; on 天穿日 (lunar 1/20,
Thursday 25 February) Grandma packs Sam's suitcase. The review is the evening before he leaves.

Lesson 50 starts the second wave: from here the learner also goes back to lesson 1 and translates it from English
(say so in a note, once).

## Voices and threads
- Mrs Liu (阿姆) comes forward: she is from 竹東 and switches to Hailu with her own family. Her brother is 阿舅
  (A-in's and A-min's maternal uncle), who runs a small 粄條 factory. In the Sixian text, the 竹東 relatives' lines are
  still given in both accents like everything else; a note says that in the story they are speaking Hailu.
- 天穿日 was promised in lesson 22's note "in its season": now is its season.
- Grandma's song (promised in lessons 37 and 42) can come at 圍爐 or on the last evening.
- A-in is home for the whole New Year week.
