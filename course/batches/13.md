# Batch 13 · Lessons 85–91 · Summer: music, the 義民 festival, the mountains

## The weeks
July and August 2027. A-in now works for the county and lives at home; Grandpa is back in the tea field, slower.
Sam visits the Hakka museum in 銅鑼 with A-min (school holidays); a dinner-table debate about whether A-min should
speak Hakka or English at home; Grandpa's old friend's 八音 band rehearses for the festival; the 義民 festival on lunar
7/20 (Saturday 21 August); then Sam and A-min go hiking above 南庄, get lost, and are found. The review is the day
after.

## Care
History (嘉應州, 義民) and religion: describe what the example sentences and MOE definitions say, label the rest
"general knowledge", and stay neutral on the contested history of the 義民. The rescue is frightening but brief, and
nobody is hurt; Grandma's reaction is the emotional centre.
