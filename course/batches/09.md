# Batch 9 · Lessons 57–63 · A month in 竹東

## The week(s)
March 2027. Sam lives above 阿舅's small 粄條 factory in 竹東 and works there; everyone around him speaks Hailu, and
Sam, trained in Sixian, keeps tripping over the differences (the batch is where the Hailu line becomes the "real" one
in the story, while both texts are still given). The lessons spread over three weeks: the first day, the colleagues, a
lunch box, the Hakka TV news in the evening, a rainy weekend, a letter to A-in. The review is his last evening
before going back to Miaoli.

| # | Scene | What the learner can say afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 57 | The first day at work | 會 for habits (逐日會…), workplace words |
| 58 | Colleagues | "while…", 越…越, 緊 X 緊 Y revisited |
| 59 | The lunch box | food, V 等來 revisited, 好 + V "easy to" |
| 60 | Hakka TV news | news register, 因為…所以 / 故所, numbers in thousands |
| 61 | A rainy weekend | V 啊 V 仔 (on and off), 試V看仔 (Hailu "try") |
| 62 | Writing to A-in | letter style, 為著, 分 + purpose |
| 63 | Review 9 | nothing new |

## Voices
- 阿舅 (Mrs Liu's brother): brisk, kind, teases Sam's Miaoli accent.
- One or two colleagues with names; one older woman who speaks only Hailu, one young man who mixes in Mandarin.
- A-in by phone and letter (lesson 62): the A-in and Sam thread moves one small step.
