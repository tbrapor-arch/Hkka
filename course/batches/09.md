# Batch 9 · Lessons 57–63 · A month in 竹東

## The week(s)
March 2027. Sam lives above 阿舅's small 粄條 factory in 竹東 and works there; everyone around him speaks Hailu, and
Sam, trained in Sixian, keeps tripping over the differences (the batch is where the Hailu line becomes the "real" one
in the story, while both texts are still given). The lessons spread over three weeks: the first day, the colleagues, a
lunch box, the Hakka TV news in the evening, a rainy weekend, a letter to A-in. The review is his last evening
before going back to Miaoli.

Calendar (2027): 57 Friday 26 February (the day after 天穿日); 58 Monday 1 March; 59 Wednesday 3 March; 60 Tuesday
9 March, evening; 61 Saturday 13 – Sunday 14 March; 62 Sunday 21 March; 63 Friday 26 March, the last evening (Sam
goes back to Miaoli on Saturday 27). A-in works in Taipei on weekdays; she phones in the evenings.

Colleagues: 阿蘭嫂 (about sixty, thirty years at the factory, Hailu only) and 阿傑 (twenty-two, half a year there).
Sam's trip-ups (checked in `v_word_parallel`): 寒 / 冷 for feeling cold (57), 飯 fan / pon and 肚飢 / 肚屎枵 (59),
落雨 / 落水 (61), 右片 / 正手片 (57), 故所 / 所以 (60); 粥 / 糜 (58) turned out not to be one. These are
differences in the word lists; Sixian examples often have the Hailu word too, so the notes must not overstate them.

## Voices
- 阿舅 (Mrs Liu's brother): brisk, kind, teases Sam's Miaoli accent.
- One or two colleagues with names; one older woman who speaks only Hailu, one young man who uses the newer words (越來越).
- A-in by phone and letter (lesson 62): the A-in and Sam thread moves one small step.
