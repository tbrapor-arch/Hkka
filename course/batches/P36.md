Why some cannot be taught as planned:
- **幾下 giaˊ**: one syllable for two characters. Lint cannot align it. Decide how to write it before using it.
- **鬥, 網, 窟, 鄰**: in the lessons they only occur inside a word that is already taught (鬥雙, 上網 and the proverb
  三日打魚四日晒網, 水窟, 鄰舍). They need a sentence where they stand alone.
- **除忒…以外, 毋單淨…還, 緊…緊…**: the lessons use and explain them (P38), but a pattern with … in the middle cannot be
  declared in `new_words`. `tools/coverage.py` will keep counting them as untaught.
- **無聊 vuˇ liauˇ**: the course already teaches the reading moˇ liauˇ (lesson 79).
- **該 goiˊ**: the list glosses it 容易, and its example sentences are for 該 ge. Leave it.

## What the new process changed (for the next batch)
- Writing a lesson took three or four runs of `draft.py` and lint. The tool's default reading is the one taught
  first, which is often not the wanted one for 著, 好, 落, 倒, 降: force the reading with `[word|reading]`.
- A word that is already taught inside a longer word (鬥雙) is cut as the longer word. If the plan wants the short
  one, give it a sentence of its own.
- A title cannot be reused: 三日打魚四日晒網 is the title of P22.
- Packets attach sentences by characters, so a word may get sentences of another word (採 → 儘採, 投 → 緣投). Read the
  sentence before using it.
