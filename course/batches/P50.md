## Why the lessons hold fewer new words now
- The plan now puts the 初級 and 中級 words first. Most of those left have no frequency (`f=0`) and one official
  sentence each: names of things (豬紅, 蝦公夾, 火焰蟲), kin terms, measure words. A scene holds 25 to 30 of them.
- More lines are official sentences (56–58%, against 40–44% before): they are safe, but each brings one new word.
- Words left for P64–P69: 35 初級 and 230 中級. That is 44 per lesson: more than the lessons hold. Either P64–P69
  go back to about 40 words (shorter official quotes, more own sentences), or some 中級 words stay untaught.

## What changed in the process
- `course/packets/skip.tsv`: three more patterns with … (見人就…, 越來越…, 限勢…還).
- The packet no longer lists replanned or skipped words as left over.
- A word must be declared in the first lesson that uses it: 鬥緊 was moved from P54 to P50.
- Words already taught inside a longer word cannot be declared alone unless they stand alone in the text
  (鬚菇 after 刨鬚菇, 寒毛 after 寒毛菇, 䟘 after 䟘床).
- Rules added: `marks-fun`, `my-body`; `zai-at` now accepts 原在.
