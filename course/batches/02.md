# Batch 2 · Lessons 8–14 · Sam's second week

## The week
A-in is gone; Sam lives in Hakka now. The week is built so that each day needs one new kind of sentence, and so that
the days connect: Grandma feeds him too much (it started in lesson 3) and by Saturday he is ill; the phone call with
A-in on Thursday is where he tells his week, which is a natural recap. On Sunday he goes out alone for the first time,
by bus to 公館, and gets it slightly wrong.

| # | Day, scene | What the learner can say afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Tuesday: Grandma's vegetable garden | "very / the most / so", colours, describe things |
| 9 | Wednesday: Grandpa reads the sky; the washing | "it's about to (rain)", hot and cold, the weather |
| 10 | Thursday evening: phone call with A-in | "with, to (someone)", "we / you (plural) / they", call someone |
| 11 | Friday: breakfast stall in town with A-min | "I like", "I'd like to", "no need", ordering |
| 12 | Saturday: Sam is ill | body, "it hurts", three ways to say "don't" |
| 13 | Sunday: the bus to 公館 | walk vs run, directions, taking the bus |
| 14 | Review: Sunday night, Sam tells Grandma about 公館 | nothing new |

## Threads carried on
- Grandma's 食飽吂 and second bowls (3, 7) → the cause of lesson 12.
- Sam's 恁仔細: by now he uses it right; others start to use it on him.
- Grandpa's one-word lines (4) → in 9 he is right about the rain, which he says in three words.
- A-min the teacher (1) → in 11 he orders for Sam and makes him say it himself.
- New false friends staged as scenes: 當 "very" (8), 走 "run" (13), 好 hau "like" (11).

## Load
17 new words in 8–13 (±3), at most 6 Hakka-only or false-friend words each. 七 and 九 still to place (8 or 9).
