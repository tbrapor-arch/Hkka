# Batch 4 · Lessons 22–28 · The earth god's opera

## The week
Early December, after the second rice harvest: the villages put on 收冬戲, the thanksgiving opera for the earth god
(伯公) and the other gods. Grandma takes Sam to the 伯公 shrine at the end of the lane, then to the opera at the
temple. Grandpa sits out in the cold to watch and catches a cold; Sam takes him to the pharmacy. Sam's sister is
coming for Christmas and he has to find her a room. A-min's classmate from lesson 17 comes round, and on Saturday,
with Grandma at the opera again, A-min cooks dinner.

The syllabus put 天穿日 at lesson 23; it is a spring festival (lunar 1/20), so the lesson is the winter 收冬戲 and
天穿日 appears in a note, which can come back in its season (spring).

## Threads
- Grandpa: 收冬戲 is the one thing that gets him out of the house (his 山歌 youth, lesson 20).
- The classmate (lesson 17), now named 阿玉, and A-min's embarrassment.
- Grandma's two bowls, inverted: the night she is out, the food is terrible.
- Sam's sister prepares the Christmas and New Year episodes.
