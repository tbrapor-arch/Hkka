# Batch 11 · Lessons 71–77 · May: the south, and a decision

## The weeks
May 2027. Sam and A-in take a long weekend in 美濃 (south Sixian): the accent is different (歸 for 轉, 𠊎等, 蓋 for
盡), there are paper umbrellas, 粄條 and the old tobacco barns. Back in Miaoli, A-in phones from Taipei about her job;
she goes to the interview; and the week ends with Sam cooking for the family to say thank you.

## Voices
- 美濃 speakers: an old couple who run the guesthouse, and the umbrella maker. Their lines are "southern Sixian"
  only where the data shows a difference; say so in a note. The Hailu line still follows the course.
- A-in in the interview: formal, careful, a register the learner hasn't heard from her.

## Load
23 new words (±3), at most 8 hard words. Follow `checklist.md`.

## As written
- 71–73 teach 蓋, 𠊎等, 歸 and 面帕粄 from the data: MOE senses (蓋 很, 歸 返回, 𠊎等 我們, 面帕粄 "六堆地區稱") and the
  bracketed alternatives of the official examples (當（蓋）about 1,300 times, 轉（歸）about 110, 𠊎兜（𠊎等）about 30).
  That these are the southern forms is stated as general knowledge.
- The "me" contraction of 毋係 is not in the data; lesson 74 teaches the attested tag 抑毋係？ and says so.
- Lesson 74: A-in has been called to interview (as revised in batch 10). Lesson 75: she passes (下晝收著上班通知，這擺个
  面試過關哩) and decides on the spot to take the job; she starts in June.
