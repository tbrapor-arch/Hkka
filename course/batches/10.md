# Batch 10 · Lessons 64–70 · April: back home, 清明, flowers

## The weeks
Sam comes back from 竹東 on Saturday 27 March 2027, his Hakka now full of Hailu, which the family teases. April
brings 清明 (Monday 5 April 2027): the family sweeps the graves (掛紙), and Grandma tells the story of Grandpa's
father on the way. Then A-min's school sports day, a day trip to 三義 to see the wood carvers, a family meeting (A-in
has been offered a job, and must choose), and, at the end of April, the 桐花 (tung blossom) in the hills. The review
is a Sunday at the end of April.
