# Batch 5 · Lessons 29–35 · Lucie's week

## The week
Sam's younger sister Lucie arrives for seven nights (lesson 25) and stays in A-in's room. She speaks no Chinese at
all, so Sam, three weeks into Hakka, becomes the family's interpreter, and Grandma talks to Lucie anyway. The family
takes her strawberry picking in 大湖; Sam posts a box of the family's tea home; A-in comes back for her birthday; the
neighbours decide Lucie is Sam's girlfriend; Lucie loses her phone; a cold wave hits on her last day. The review is
the farewell at 苗栗 station, where lesson 1 began.

The syllabus put a typhoon at lesson 34; typhoons do not come in December, so the lesson is a cold wave (寒流) with
a strong wind. The focus (會 for what will happen, 分 for the passive, 驚) is unchanged.

| # | Scene | What the learner can say afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 29 | Strawberry picking in 大湖 | "holding / wearing… (V等)", "keep on doing (緊V)", counting fruit |
| 30 | The post office | send something to someone (寄…分…), give someone something |
| 31 | A-in's birthday | presents: 送分, lend: 借分, birthdays and ages |
| 32 | The neighbours talk | "I heard that…" (聽講), "exactly!" (係毋係), "here it is!" |
| 33 | Lucie's phone | lose (跌忒), look for and find (尋, 尋著), finish (食忒, 講煞, 洗好) |
| 34 | The cold wave | "it's going to…", "got blown away" (分風吹走), being afraid |
| 35 | Review: the station | nothing new |

## Voices
- **Lucie**: speaks French only (Sam is French, as in Tsheh-tû); her few lines are in French, and Sam's Hakka
  renderings of them carry the dialogue. She never speaks Hakka, except one word at the end (恁仔細, of course).
- Sam: now the competent one, for the first time; he still gets things wrong.

## Load
Stage B begins: 21 new words per lesson (±3), at most 6 Hakka-only or false-friend words. Longer lines, linked
clauses. Follow `checklist.md`.
