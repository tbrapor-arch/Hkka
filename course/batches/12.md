# Batch 12 · Lessons 78–84 · June: Grandpa

## The weeks
June 2027. Grandpa has a fall in the tea field and spends a week in hospital in 苗栗. Visiting hours bring the
neighbours and their manners; Grandma calls in to a Hakka radio programme to thank the nurses; Sam reads a Hakka
poem to Grandpa in hospital; once Grandpa is home, Grandma teaches Sam a 山歌; and the family looks through an old
photo album, Grandpa and Grandma's wedding fifty years ago. The review is Grandpa's first evening back on the
veranda.

## Tone
Serious but not grim: Grandpa is never in danger, and his silences become a running joke with the nurses. Any poem
or song written for the course is said to be so in its note, with the rhyme checked on Sixian finals. A-in comes home
from Taipei for the weekend of 5 June.
