# Batch 14 · Lessons 92–98 · Autumn, and the end of the year

## The weeks
September to November 2027. Mid-Autumn on the terrace (Wednesday 15 September): Grandpa and Grandma's contest of 師傅話;
in October A-in persuades Sam to enter the county Hakka speech contest, and they write the speech together; the
contest (Saturday 23 October); the second rice harvest (Saturday 6 November); Sam's goodbye to Grandma (Wednesday 24
November); Friday 26 November, exactly a year after lesson 1, the same platform at 苗栗 station. The review is on the
train to the airport the next morning.
