# Grammar inventory for Mandarin speakers

What a learner who speaks Mandarin at B2 actually has to learn in Sixian Hakka, grouped by topic, with the stage where
the course introduces it. Same layout as Tsheh-tû's `course/grammar-inventory.md`.

- **Pinyin**: MOE Hakka pinyin, Sixian, citation tones, taken from `data/build/hakka.db` (115年度 certification list,
  else the MOE dictionary). "—" = not in either source; the reading is from the cited article.
- **#**: frequency rank among the 7,890 Sixian certification words (Glossika × 10 + corpus, `v_core` ordering).
- **Src**: the MOE 客語知識庫 article the point is based on (`docs/sources.md` › moe-knowledge-articles; text in
  `Hkka-private/reference/moe/articles-text/`). Everything below is our own summary; examples are short illustrations,
  most of them standard phrases quoted in those articles. Lessons write their own sentences.
- **HL**: the Hailu (海陸) form when it differs, for the lines written alongside (from `v_word_parallel` and the articles).

**Stages** (see `pacing.md`): **A** = lessons 1–27 (survival core: about 430 words) · **B** = lessons 29–48 (longer
sentences, linking clauses, complements, to about 800 words) · **C** = lessons 50–100 (dense dialogues, register,
idioms and proverbs, the fine points of 會, 好, 正, 恁, 過).

## How the vocabulary splits for this learner
Of the 3,030 core words (115年度 基礎級 + 初級 + 中級, Sixian):
- **1,394 same as Mandarin**: only the reading is new. Taught through sound correspondences (Lesson 0) and recycled.
- **485 Hakka-only** (𠊎, 佢, 个, 毋, 吂, 恁, 屋下…): most grammar words are here. These get the notes.
- **367 same characters, different use**: possible false friends (當 = 很, 愛 = 要, 該 = 那, 走 = 跑). Flagged every time.
- **784 partial** (做得 = 可以, 今晡日 = 今天…), easy to guess once seen.
In the 100 most frequent words, 68 are not plain Mandarin. That is where the time goes.

## 3. The negation system · Stage A (core), B (fine points)
Mandarin 不 / 沒 / 別 / 還沒 split into seven Hakka words.

| Hakka | Pinyin | Roughly | # | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 毋 | mˇ | 不 | 39 | will and plain "not" before verbs: 毋去 "won't go", 毋知 "don't know". Never before a noun |
| 無 | moˇ | 沒 / 沒有 | 6 | absence (無錢), event didn't happen (無去), with 過 (無去過). *毋去過 is impossible |
| 無愛 / 嫑 | moˇ oi / moiˊ | 不要 | 773 | "don't want"; 嫑 is the contraction |
| 莫 | mog | 別 | 481 | sharp "don't!": 莫食 |
| 毋好 | mˇ hoˋ | 不要、不可以 | 55 | advice or prohibition: 壞事毋好做 |
| 毋使 | mˇ siiˋ | 不必 | 84 | "no need": 毋使愁 "don't worry" |
| 吂 | mangˇ | 還沒 | 50 | 佢吂(有)去臺北 "he hasn't been to Taipei yet"; also sentence-final question: 食吂？ "eaten yet?" (南四縣 毋曾) |

The contraction **毋係 → me (55)** (one syllable) is everywhere in speech: 這毋係！ "here it is!", 係毋係 "exactly!",
還毋係 / 就毋係 "it's just that…", 你毋知毋係 "you didn't know, did you". Stage B. *Src: 110_詞分_023, 110_036.*

## 4. Wanting, can, may, know · Stage A–B
| Hakka | Pinyin | Mandarin | # | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 愛 | oi | 要、需要 | 9 | **false friend**: "want / need / have to", not "love". 𠊎愛去 "I want to / have to go" |
| 想 | xiongˋ | 想 | 36 | |
| 好 (hau) | hau | 喜歡 | 17 | "to like" (去聲). Distinct from 好 hoˋ "good" |
| 惜 | xiagˋ | 疼、愛惜 | 586 | to love, cherish (people) |
| 做得 / 毋做得 | zo dedˋ | 可以 / 不可以 | 30 | permission and possibility |
| 會 | voi | 會 | 14 | see below |
| 識 | siidˋ | 曾經 | 306 | experience: 阿婆識坐過飛行機 "grandma has been on a plane"; 毋識 = 沒…過 |
| 曉得 | hiauˋ dedˋ | 會、懂得 | 755 | learned skill: 曉得講客話 |
| 知 | diˊ | 知道 | 94 | 毋知 "don't know" |

**會** has four uses, two of them unknown to Mandarin (Stage A for 1–2, B for 3, C for 4):
1. lasting ability (會泅水) and habit (逐日會去公園);
2. 當會 = "good at": 三伯姆當會炊粄; the greeting **恁會早** "you're up early!";
3. prediction (甜个莫放桌頂，會餳蟻公 "…or ants will come");
4. **"about to"**: 會落雨咧 "it's about to rain", 厥爸會八十歲 "his father is nearly eighty". Not Mandarin 會.

**好 hoˋ + verb**: "may / now we can" (好食飯哩 "dinner's ready, let's eat"), "so that" (買轉來𠊎好煮菜), "easy to"
(好使 "handy"). Stage B. *Src: 112_029, 112_030, 111_018, 111_019.*

## 6. 同 / 摎, 分 and other prepositions · Stage A (basic), B (full)
**同 tungˇ / 摎 lauˊ** (same functions; 同 is older Sixian, 摎 spreading from Hailu, now common in northern Sixian):
- "and": 阿英摎阿姨共下去街頂;
- "with, to": 摎阿明講話;
- "from": 摎佢借錢 "borrow money from him";
- **disposal = 把**: 摎杯仔打爛了 "broke the cup";
- "for (someone's benefit)": 摎厥倈仔買一領新衫.

**分 funˊ** is "give" and grows into four grammar words:
- give: 佢分𠊎一支筆;
- "to" after another verb: 送一支筆分𠊎, 借錢分佢;
- **let**: 佢會分阿英去台北 "he'll let A-in go to Taipei";
- **passive = 被**: 佢分𠊎打;
- **purpose "so that"**: 賺錢分細人仔讀書, 唱條山歌分你聽.

Double objects: 分 + person + thing (阿姆分佢錢) or V + thing + 分 + person (阿姆借錢分佢) or V分 + person + thing
(借分佢錢). The order **分 + thing + person (阿姆分錢佢) is Hailu only**: wrong in Sixian.
Others: 對 dui (#40) "toward, to", 跈 tenˇ "follow, go along with", 到 (see 2).
*Src: 108_031, 108_030, 111_025, 109_028, 112_033.*

## 9. Degree and comparison · Stage A–B
| Hakka | Pinyin | Mandarin | # | Note | HL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 當 | dongˊ | 很 | 7 | **false friend**; the everyday "very" | |
| 盡 | qin | 最 (and 很) | 121 | in Sixian 盡 is "most"; 最 is spreading | 最 zuiˇ |
| 已 | iˊ | 很 | – | | |
| 還 | hanˇ | 好、挺 | 26 | 還好食！ "really tasty!" | |
| 恁 | anˋ | 那麼 | 51 | | |
| 忒 | tedˋ | 太 | 76 | 食忒飽 "ate too much" | 昶 |
| 較 | ka | 比較、更 | 35 | | 較 haˇ |
| 死 + adj | xiˋ | 超級 | – | 死鹹 "terribly salty", 死硬 | |

Fixed intensifiers, one per adjective (Stage C, vocabulary): 浸青, 啾紅, 淰黃, 蓬白, 律圓, 繃絚 ("deep green, bright red…").

Comparison (Stage B):
- **X 比 Y 較 A** (the main pattern): 佢比你較高 (and 佢比你高五公分 with an amount);
- X A 過 Y: 佢高過你, 好過 (only with one-syllable adjectives in Sixian; freer in Hailu);
- X A Y + amount: 佢高你五公分;
- same / different: A 摎 B 共樣 / 無共樣 (kiung iong);
- "as … as": A 有 B 恁 A, A 無 B A (阿英無阿明高).
*Src: 112_006, 108_028, 109_035, 108_027.*

## 10. Word order: where Hakka differs from Mandarin · Stage B
- **Adverb after the verb**: 食加一碗飯 "have one more bowl", 食多兜仔 "eat a bit more", 著少一領衫 "wear one layer
  less", **你行先** "you go first", 走往出 "run outside". Mandarin order (多食一碗, 先走) is also heard; teach the
  Hakka order first.
- **Sentence-final 添** tiamˊ "more, again": 食一碗添 "have another bowl".
- **V (一) CL N 來** for a casual, small action: 睡一覺目來 "have a nap", 食碗飯來 "grab a bite".
- **Object inside the potential complement**: 看佢得著 / 看佢毋著 "can / can't see him" (newer: 看得著佢).
  食飯毋落 "can't eat any more".
- **Complement of degree with 到** do: 寫字寫到盡正板 "writes very neatly"; 驚到… "so scared that…".
*Src: 109_029, 109_030, 108_026.*

## 11. Sentence-final particles · Stage A (無, 吂, 了, 啊), B (rest)
- Yes/no questions with **無** at the end: 你有紅包袋仔無？, 好無？ "OK?", 係無？ "is it?";
- 吂？ "yet?";
- **了 / 咧** leˊ "now / already" (change of state);
- **哩** "(it's) time to": 好食飯哩;
- 喔 / 哦, 呢 neˇ, 啊: tone and softening (the course uses them sparingly and explains each);
- **正** at the end = "(do it) later": 有閒正來尞 "come and visit when you're free", 下二擺正 "next time, then".
*Src: 111_020, 110_036.*

## 14. Everyday nouns that differ; word formation · Stage A–B
屋下 vugˋ kaˊ (home), 今晡日 gimˊ buˊ ngidˋ (today), 昨晡日, 天光日 (tomorrow; HL 韶早), 朝晨 (morning), 暗晡
(evening), 細人仔 (child), 倈仔 (son), 妹仔 (daughter), 阿爸, 阿姆 (mother), 阿公, 阿婆, 老弟, 老妹, 餔娘 (wife),
老公 (husband). There is no Hakka 老婆: a wife is 餔娘 or 老姐.

Affixes (Stage B, as notes):
- 阿- for kin and names (阿哥, 阿玉妹);
- 老- for same-generation kin (老弟, 老妹);
- -仔 for "small / familiar" or just a noun ending (桌仔, 凳仔, 阿英仔);
- -公, -牯, -嫲, -婆, -哥 lose their gender in many words: 蝦公 "shrimp", 刀嫲 "knife", 石牯 "stone";
- -晡, -頭 in time words: 暗晡頭, 朝晨頭.

The 仔 suffix copies the end of the syllable before it: 豬仔 zuˊ veˋ, 柑仔 gamˊ meˋ, 桌仔 zogˋ geˋ, 鐵仔 tiedˋ leˋ
(see 16 for its tone). *Src: 109_032, 112_004, 112_005.*

## 15. Reduplication · Stage B–C
Adjectives:
- AA stronger: 紅紅;
- AA仔 milder: 黃黃仔 "yellowish";
- AAA strongest: 遠遠遠 "really far";
- ABB vivid: 紅冬冬 fungˇ dungˊ dungˊ, 白雪雪;
- ABAB mild vs AABB strong: 三八三八 / 三三八八.

Verbs:
- VV啊 (see 5);
- -仔 after reduplicated adverbs: 慢慢仔 "slowly", 久久仔 "once in a while".
*Src: 109_031, 107_020.*

## 16. Sound system topics (Lesson 0, then spiralled)
- 17 initials (counting the zero initial), no zh/ch/sh; the most finals of any Taiwanese Hakka, including -ii and the
  Sixian-only -iim, -iin, -iib, -iid (針 ziimˊ, 陳 ciinˇ, 汁 ziibˋ, 直 ciid); stops -b -d -g.
- Six tones: 陰平 ˊ24, 陽平 ˇ11, 上聲 ˋ31, 去聲 55 (unmarked), 陰入 ˋ2, 陽入 5 (unmarked).
- **One tone sandhi rule**: 陰平 becomes ˇ before 陰平, 去聲 and 陽入 (天光 tienˇ gongˊ, 添飯, 三月), inside a phrase.
  Plus the 仔 rule: eˋ is read eˇ after 上聲 or 陰入 (草仔, 鐵仔). Implemented in `tools/sandhi.py`.
- Literary and colloquial readings (文白異讀) where Mandarin knowledge misleads.
*Src: 107_012, 107_013, 107_014, 107_015; MOE workbook lesson 25.*

## 17. Writing Hailu alongside: the differences that matter
| Point | Sixian | Hailu |
|---|---|---|
